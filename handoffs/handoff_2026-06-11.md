### Task A -- calibration- and ROI-aware health verdict (`main.py`)

Added three fields to `GET /model-health` per system:
- `flags`: list -- `inverted`, `miscalibrated`, `negative_roi`, `underpowered`, `no_edge`
- `health`: composite verdict with precedence: underpowered -> inverted -> miscalibrated -> no_edge -> degraded -> moderate -> healthy
- `recommended_action`: human string keyed to health verdict

Module-level constants (env-overridable): `MIN_HEALTH_N=20`, `CAL_ERR_TOL=0.10`, `ROI_FLOOR=-10`.
Existing `signal` field unchanged (backward-compatible).
