## The core finding (drives everything above)

The run-sim spike + /edge-analysis proved: **the models are NOT broken; bet
selection is.** On the full OOS holdout the v18 binary ensemble has AUC 0.62 and is
well-calibrated. The ~0.50 AUC / -0.26 cal_err seen in /model-health are
SELECTION-BIAS artifacts of the censored bets table. Realized_cal_err scales with
gap size (+0.03 at <5% -> -0.31 at >=20%): the biggest prediction-to-odds gaps are
model overconfidence, not edge. Calibration (Task #3) attacks this directly.

Post-CLV-fix corroboration: F5's >=20% bucket now shows CLV -9.9% alongside ROI
-24.6% (previously an incoherent +59% CLV). ROI and CLV now AGREE that the big-gap
bets are genuinely bad selection, not variance.
